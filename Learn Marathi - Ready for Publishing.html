🧱 Sentence Building — वाक्य बनवा

Marathi uses Subject → Object → Verb (SOV). English: "I eat rice" → Marathi: "I rice eat" = मी भात खातो!

Subject + Object + Verb
मी (I)भात (rice)खातो (eat)
मी भात खातो.
Mi bhaat khaato.
I eat rice.
Subject + Object + Verb
ती (She)पुस्तक (book)वाचते (reads)
ती पुस्तक वाचते.
Ti pustak vaachte.
She reads a book.
Subject + Place + Verb
आम्ही (We)शाळेत (to school)जातो (go)
आम्ही शाळेत जातो.
Aamhi shaalet jaato.
We go to school.
Subject + "ला" (to/for) + Noun + Verb
मला (To me)आंबा (mango)आवडतो (like)
मला आंबा आवडतो.
Mala aambaa aavadto.
I like mango.

📖 Longer Story — मोठी गोष्ट
🐢🐇 कासव आणि ससा — The Tortoise and the Hare
🐇
एक ससा होता. तो खूप वेगाने धावत असे.
Ek sasaa hotaa. To khoop vegaane dhaavat ase.
There was a hare. He ran very fast.
🐢
एक कासव होते. ते हळू हळू चालत असे.
Ek kaasav hote. Te halu halu chaalat ase.
There was a tortoise. It walked slowly.
🗣️
ससा म्हणाला, "मी तुझ्यापेक्षा वेगवान आहे!"
Sasaa mhanalaa, "Mi tuzhyaapekshaa vegvaan aahe!"
The hare said, "I am faster than you!"
🐢
कासव म्हणाले, "चल, शर्यत लावूया!"
Kaasav mhanaale, "Chal, sharyat laavuya!"
The tortoise said, "Let's have a race!"
🏁
शर्यत सुरू झाली! ससा पुढे पळाला.
Sharyat suru zhaali! Sasaa pudhe palaalaa.
The race started! The hare ran ahead.
😴
ससा विचार करू लागला, "कासव मागे आहे." तो झाडाखाली झोपला!
Sasaa vichaar karu laagalaa, "Kaasav maage aahe." To zhaadaakhaali zhoplaa!
The hare thought, "Tortoise is far behind." He slept under a tree!
🐢
कासव चालतच राहिले. हळू पण सतत.
Kaasav chaalatach raahile. Halu pan satat.
The tortoise kept walking. Slow but steady.
🏆
कासवाने शर्यत जिंकली! ससा उठला तेव्हा खूप उशीर झाला होता.
Kaasavaane sharyat jinkli! Sasaa uthlaa tevhaa khoop usheer zhaalaa hotaa.
The tortoise won! When the hare woke up, it was too late.
बोधकथा (Moral):
हळू हळू पण सतत चालणारा जिंकतो!
Halu halu pan satat chaalnaaraa jinkto!
Slow and steady wins the race! 🐢🏆

🌟 Tips for Parents — All Levels

  • Point & Say: Point at objects and say the Marathi word. Repeat daily!
  • Sing Along: Marathi rhymes like "ये रे ये रे पावसा" make learning musical.
  • Counting Game: Count toys, stairs in Marathi — एक, दोन, तीन!
  • Color Hunt: "Find something लाल (red)!" — learning as a game.
  • Daily Phrases: Use greetings (नमस्कार, धन्यवाद) every day.
  • Act It Out: Say "नाचणे!" and dance, "धावणे!" and run!
  • Storytelling: Read the stories aloud with expressions.
  • Quiz Time: Use the interactive quizzes — kids love tapping answers!
  • Matching Game: Play together for word recognition practice.
  • Sentence Building: Use the color blocks — Subject + Object + Verb!
  • Festival Fun: During Ganeshotsav, teach "गणपती बाप्पा मोरया!"
  • Daily Routine: Narrate the child's day in Marathi.
  • Writing: Print trace cards — start with अ, आ, क, म.
